## 研究问题 / The Research Question

冷战末期，美国和苏联面对的是**同一批技术条件**——相控阵雷达已经成熟，数字数据链可以实用化，空空导弹和地空导弹开始装备主动雷达导引头——为什么两边最终走出的路径差别如此之大？

At the end of the Cold War, the United States and the Soviet Union faced **the same set of technological conditions**: phased-array radars had matured, digital data links had become practical, and air-to-air and surface-to-air missiles were beginning to be fitted with active radar homing seekers. Why did the two sides ultimately pursue such different paths?

1991 年苏联解体使这场比较被迫中断。本研究用反事实推演补全被截断的另一半，再以苏联解体后俄罗斯体系的真实退化过程作双向对照。

The dissolution of the Soviet Union in 1991 forcibly interrupted this comparison. This study uses counterfactual deduction to reconstruct the truncated half, then cross-checks the results in two directions against the actual degradation of the Russian system after the collapse.

## 研究方法 / Method

| 方法 Method | 说明 Description |
| --- | --- |
| **历史比较**<br>Historical comparison | 按时间顺序重建美苏双方在 1991 年前后已具备的技术条件，严格依据公开文献，不做推测。<br>Reconstructing chronologically the technological conditions both sides already possessed around 1991, strictly from published sources and without speculation. |
| **反事实推演**<br>Counterfactual deduction | 遵循 Lebow 的操作规范，设定**技术继承链、工程周期、体系哲学延续**三项约束，推演冷战延续情境下双方到 2026 年的体系形态。<br>Following Lebow's operational norms, with three constraints — technological inheritance chains, engineering cycles, and continuity of system design philosophy — projecting both sides' systems to 2026. |
| **双向验证**<br>Two-directional verification | 将推演结果与俄罗斯体系的真实退化过程对照，重点检验被判定为"受阻"的环节。<br>Cross-checking the deduction against Russia's actual degradation, with particular attention to links judged to be blocked. |

分析采用**四层对比框架**（传感器 / 数据链 / 武器 / 指挥控制）与**闭环完整性度量**（A 锁 — B 射 — C 导三要素能否贯通）两项工具。

The analysis uses two instruments: a **four-layer comparative framework** (sensors / data links / weapons / command and control) and a **measure of closed-loop completeness** (whether the three elements A-locks, B-shoots, C-guides run through the engagement).

## 主要发现 / Key Findings

**1. 1991 年的差距远小于通常叙述。** 苏联防空体系在架构层面已具备探测与火力分离的骨架，短板在数据精度与指挥层级时延，而非体系设想本身。

*The 1991 gap was far smaller than the standard narrative suggests.* At the architectural level the Soviet air defense system already possessed the skeleton of detection–firepower separation; its weaknesses lay in data precision and latency across command echelons, not in the system concept itself.

**2. 美国的优势集中在一项工程能力。** 即把地理分散的雷达数据合成为单一火控级航迹图。

*The American advantage was concentrated in a single engineering capability*: fusing geographically dispersed radar data into one fire-control-quality track picture.

**3. 体系设计逻辑是一个独立解释变量。** 它与技术水平高低并不等同；该变量由作战想定、军种结构与工业体制共同塑造，一旦形成即具路径依赖性。

*System design logic is an independent explanatory variable*, not equivalent to the level of technological capability. It is shaped jointly by operational scenario, service structure, and industrial system, and once formed it is strongly path-dependent.

**4. 一个反直觉命题。** 在双方均保有二次打击能力、且对彼此客场劣势判断准确时，协同交战能力若在两个超级大国之间同步成熟，其净效应可能是**抑制**而非鼓励先发制人。本文说明了该命题不再成立的三项条件。

*A counter-intuitive proposition.* Where both sides retain a second-strike capability and judge each other's away-theater disadvantages accurately, the simultaneous maturation of cooperative engagement capability between two superpowers may have a net effect of **inhibiting** rather than encouraging preemption. The three conditions under which it ceases to hold are specified.
